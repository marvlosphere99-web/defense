About

Baseline "normal" behaviour is learned live via an EWMA mean/variance per traffic metric (packets/sec, bytes/sec, avg packet size, unique destination ports). Each new sample gets a z-score against that baseline; a composite anomaly score is the largest absolute z-score across metrics. The baseline only updates on ticks that aren't flagged, so a sustained attack can't smooth itself into "normal." A separate detector tracks the regularity (coefficient of variation) of new-connection timing to catch beaconing — a pattern that's deliberately invisible to the magnitude-based z-scores, to demonstrate why one technique alone isn't enough.
